## [0.1.0] - 2026-02-19

### Added

#### Core Framework
- `Lattice` class for discrete spacetime structures with periodic boundary conditions
- `EvolutionOperator` for quantum and classical dynamics
- `InformationTheory` tools for entropy, mutual information, and complexity measures

#### Quantum Modules
- `QuantumFlow` for simulating quantum information propagation
- `QuantumState` for pure and mixed state representation
- `EntanglementMeasures` including concurrence, negativity, and entanglement of formation
- Quantum gate implementations (Hadamard, CNOT)
- Entangling circuit generation

#### Emergent Systems
